# Program Overview
## VulnOptiCON 2026

This year, we’re introducing an additional day to cover All Things Vulns! This one-day mini event will take place on Day 0 of VulnOptiCON. It will provide a hub for the European vulnerability community to network and share information broadly relating to the vulnerabilities and exploits ecosystem. Whether it's about the future of vulnerability management, the anticipated AI vulnpocalypse, the realities of building a VDP, researcher legal consideration, challenges of being a CNA or any other security vulnerabilities topic, we want to hear from you!
